# The mark

`src/mark.svg` is the Wikidata barcode from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 15.0.0
(`wikidata.svg`, CC0 1.0), with its `role` and `<title>` removed and `aria-hidden` added. It is
drawn in one colour, as a mask in the text colour, to show where awards come from. Wikidata's
data is CC0.
