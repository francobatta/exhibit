# The marks

Each is drawn in one colour, as a mask in the text colour, to show whose score or which service a
line comes from. They are used for identification only and are trademarks of their owners.

| File | Shown for | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `src/mark.svg` | MDBList (Settings) | Drawn by hand: an M in a rounded square, after the site's initials. Not an official asset. | — |
| `src/marks/imdb.svg` | IMDb | [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 15.0.0, `imdb.svg` | CC0 1.0 |
| `src/marks/tomatometer.svg` | Tomatometer | Simple Icons 15.0.0, `rottentomatoes.svg` | CC0 1.0 |
| `src/marks/metacritic.svg` | Metacritic | Simple Icons 15.0.0, `metacritic.svg` | CC0 1.0 |
| `src/marks/letterboxd.svg` | Letterboxd average | The Letterboxd adapter's own hand-drawn mark (`crates/exhibit-letterboxd`) | — |
| `src/marks/popcornmeter.svg` | Popcornmeter | Drawn by hand: a popcorn bucket. Not an official asset. | — |

Simple Icons' files had their `role` and `<title>` removed and `aria-hidden` added.

## Attribution

Rotten Tomatoes asks that its scores carry its name and a link. Exhibit has no browser on the
TV, so every Tomatometer and Popcornmeter score is shown with its mark and named in words on
Details, where the page's address (`rottentomatoes.com/m/…`) is written out beside the score.
